UrbanFood – E-commerce Platform

UrbanFood is a web-based e-commerce platform that connects urban farmers and local producers with consumers seeking fresh food and handmade products.
This project was developed as part of a Take Home Assessment to demonstrate full-stack development, relational and non-relational database integration, and real-world e-commerce workflows.

📌 Project Overview

UrbanFood provides a marketplace where customers can browse products, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries. The system supports both relational database operations (Oracle) for core business logic and NoSQL (MongoDB) for handling user-generated content such as reviews and feedback.

🎯 Features

 Customer Management – Sign up, login, manage profiles

 Product & Supplier Management – Add, update, delete products and suppliers

 Order Processing – Place orders with multiple products per order

 Payment Handling – Manage payments, statuses, and methods

 Delivery Management – Track deliveries for completed orders

 User Reviews & Feedback (MongoDB) – Customers can add reviews stored in NoSQL DB

📊 Reports & Analytics (Oracle PL/SQL) –

Find high-demand products

Calculate total sales over specific periods
